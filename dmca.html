2. Procedure for Reporting Infringement

If you are a copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of one, and you believe that any content hosted or linked on our site infringes your copyrights, please send a written DMCA notice containing the following details to our support email:

  • Identification: A detailed description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed, including titles or direct references.
  • Infringing Material: The exact location/URLs on our website containing the material you wish to have removed.
  • Contact Info: Your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  • Good Faith Statement: A statement by you that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • Accuracy Statement: A statement by you, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the copyright owner's behalf.
  • Signature: An electronic or physical signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the copyright interest.

3. Submitting the Notice

Please send the complete, signed written notice to: dmca@hxhreader.com

Upon receipt of a valid and complete DMCA notice, we will investigate and remove the identified content or link within 48-72 business hours. Please note that we do not host any images directly on our server; the web app acts as a viewer proxy and scraper cache for public CDN mirrors.
